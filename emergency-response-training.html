Emergency Response Training

Emergency Preparedness
Client
Public health institution
Scope
20+ course eLearning curriculum
Tools
Rise, Storyline
Need

The COVID-19 pandemic exposed gaps in emergency readiness. A public health institution needed a training program to prepare healthcare professionals to respond in emergency situations, and it needed to be delivered on a tight timeline.

Approach

Partnered with project leaders, subject matter experts, and a team of developers to build a full eLearning curriculum.

Mapped the eLearning curriculum with project leaders.

Worked with subject matter experts to gather and shape the content.

Led a team of developers to build more than 20 courses in three months.

Designed hands-on simulations, including a mass casualty triage exercise where learners sort and assess victims using the SALT triage method.

Led a development team using Articulate Rise and Storyline.

Outcome
4.6 / 5
average course rating from learners

The program launched in summer 2022, and courses averaged a 4.6 out of 5 rating. The client continues to use the modules in ongoing training sessions.
