Facilitation Skills for Lifestyle Coaches

Diabetes Prevention
Client
Emory University, DTTAC
Scope
Interactive web-based course
Tools
Storyline, Illustrator, Canva
Need

Emory University’s Diabetes Training and Technical Assistance Center (DTTAC) trains lifestyle coaches to deliver diabetes prevention programs. New coaches needed a strong foundation in facilitation, but had limited time for training. DTTAC needed a short, highly engaging course that would prepare them quickly.

Approach

Partnered with client leadership and subject matter experts to design an interactive web-based course built for busy learners.

Defined the audience, course goals, and learning objectives with the client team.

Used a “guide for the journey” visual metaphor to show the shift from teaching to facilitating.

Brought DTTAC’s Three Rs of facilitation to life through illustrated characters and scenarios.

Built a toolkit of nine facilitation techniques that learners explore at their own pace.

Designed practice activities where learners sort teaching and facilitating behaviors, then spot facilitation techniques in a simulated virtual meeting.

Led a development team using Articulate Storyline, Adobe Illustrator, and Canva.

Outcome
2023
launched in October and still in use today

The course launched in October 2023 and is still in use today.

The client valued its interactivity and expanded it to additional programs.
